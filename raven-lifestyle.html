How is this calculated?
Your Raven Score blends three habits from the current month: spending discipline (40%) rewards keeping bills under ~60% of income, bill timeliness (35%) tracks what's paid on or before its due date, and investing consistency (25%) measures contributions against a 15%-of-income target.
