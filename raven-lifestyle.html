How is this calculated?
Your Raven Score blends three habits from the current month: spending discipline (40%) rewards keeping bills under ~60% of income, bill timeliness (35%) tracks what's paid on or before its due date, and investing consistency (25%) measures contributions against your 15%-of-income target (default 15% — a good baseline for a healthy score, editable under Raven Suggests). Score 90+ for the whole month and a medal joins your roost.
