This Privacy Policy explains what information RAVEN collects, how we use it, and how our SMS program works.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information, including mobile phone numbers, with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

All SMS opt-out requests are honored promptly.

We only use your information to operate RAVEN, deliver requested notifications, and support your account.

Information We Collect

  • Account information such as your email address, first name, last name, and optional phone number.
  • Bill, receipt, and trip information you create inside RAVEN.
  • Payment handles you choose to store, such as Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, or Apple Pay contact details.
  • Technical information needed to secure and operate the service.

How We Use Information

  • To create and manage bills, trips, comments, receipts, and payment reminders.
  • To send transactional emails and transactional SMS messages you request or consent to receive.
  • To provide support, prevent abuse, and maintain service reliability.
  • To power optional AI features, described below, when you choose to use them.

AI Features (Receipt Scan, RAVENbot & RAVEN Suggests)

RAVEN uses Anthropic's Claude AI to power three optional features:

  • Receipt Scan — when you photograph or upload a receipt, that image is sent to Anthropic so it can read the items, prices, tax, and tip and fill them into your bill automatically.
  • RAVENbot — when you send a message to RAVENbot, the text of that message (and recent RAVENbot conversation history, so it has context) is sent to Anthropic so it can generate a reply about your trip or bill.
  • RAVEN Suggests — in RAVEN Lifestyle, when you request AI coaching, a summary of your logged paychecks, bills, and investments for that month (income and spending totals, timeliness, and investing figures) is sent to Anthropic so it can generate personalized suggestions. This does not include your name, email, phone number, or account numbers.

What is sent: only the specific receipt image, the specific message you submit, or the monthly financial summary described above — plus the minimal trip/bill context needed to answer (such as item names and amounts already on that bill). We do not send your full account history, contacts, payment details, or other trips.

What is not sent: your email, phone number, saved payment handles, account numbers, or any bill/trip you have not actively scanned or messaged about.

Your control: all three features are covered by a single "Receipt Scanning, RAVENbot & RAVEN Suggests" toggle in Settings → Privacy in the app. Nothing is sent to Anthropic until you explicitly allow it — the first time you use any of these features, RAVEN shows a permission prompt naming Anthropic and explaining what will be sent, and no data leaves your device for these features until you tap Allow. You can turn this off at any time, which disables all three features immediately, and turn it back on whenever you like.

Anthropic processes this data solely to generate the requested output (parsed receipt items, a chat reply, or coaching suggestions) and does not use it to serve you ads.

SMS Privacy

Program name: RAVEN bill and payment notifications

Message type: Transactional text messages related to bills, trips, payment reminders, payment confirmations, and account activity.

Message frequency: Varies based on your activity and the bills or trips you participate in.

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out: Reply STOP at any time.

Help: Reply HELP or contact support@ravensplit.com.

Mobile Information Sharing

RAVEN does not share mobile information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may share limited mobile information with service providers only as necessary to operate the service, such as Twilio for SMS delivery. These providers may not use that information for their own marketing purposes.

Consent for Text Messaging

Users opt in to receive transactional SMS from RAVEN during account creation or bill onboarding by entering their phone number and checking an unchecked consent checkbox.

The checkbox states that users agree to receive transactional SMS from RAVEN about bills, payment reminders, and account activity, that message frequency varies, that message and data rates may apply, that users can reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help, and that consent is not required to purchase.

Users may also opt in when joining a shared bill or trip flow that presents the same SMS disclosure and consent language.

Third-Party Service Providers

  • Twilio for SMS delivery
  • Supabase for database and authentication infrastructure
  • Anthropic for Receipt Scan (reading receipt photos you submit), RAVENbot (generating replies to messages you send), and RAVEN Suggests (generating coaching from your monthly financial summary), as described above. You can disable all three in Settings → Privacy.

These providers only receive information needed to perform their services for RAVEN.

Data Retention

We keep account and bill-related information for as long as needed to operate your account and maintain your history, unless you request deletion or we need to retain certain records for security or legal reasons.

Your Rights

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information. You can also delete your account directly from the app at Settings → Delete My Account, which permanently removes your profile and signs you out.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact us at support@ravensplit.com.
